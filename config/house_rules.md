## House rules
- The goal is to make money. A connection only matters if it leads to a bet that can be checked against prices.
- Memory holds what the news feed cannot show: theses, what each is waiting for, rules that were tested, and why.
  Do not store prices, positions or "current status" prose; those are fetched live and go stale.
- A fuse is a hypothesis plus the specific evidence that would confirm or kill it. Prefer few sharp fuses to many vague ones.
- Starting hypothesis, not a rule: edge is likelier in slow, physical, under-read developments than in headlines every desk
  is watching. The track record decides. If the record shows another kind of connection pays, follow the record.
- Scored bets are the only way this system learns. A shadow bet (conviction 0) costs nothing and is scored like a real one,
  so record one whenever you have a specific, falsifiable view, and tag what kind of connection it is.
- Say what would prove a thesis wrong before betting on it. Mark facts as checked or unconfirmed, with the source.
- The record so far (old system): single-stock picks roughly matched the energy sector, and shorts lost money.
